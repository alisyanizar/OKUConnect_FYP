          How to use this site

          Getting an answer

          1. Step 1

            Go to AI Chatbot.

          2. Step 2

            Choose a suggested question, or type your own question.

          Tips

          • Ask short questions, one at a time.
          • Use the Tab key to move around the page. Press Enter to choose.
          • Screen readers will read each new answer for you.
          • Use Text size and High contrast at the top of the page.
          • You can change the language or page at any time. Your conversation stays.
          • Do not type your IC number or other private details.
